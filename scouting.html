Scouting Experience

Eagle Scout — Auburn, NH

Earned: August 2022

Becoming an Eagle Scout taught me the importance of perseverance, teamwork, and service. Over the years, Scouting gave me opportunities to lead, teach, and make a lasting impact in my community.

  • Organized and led a multi-thousand-dollar community project to build an outdoor story walk for the Auburn Town Library, which remains in use today.
  • Served as a Junior Assistant Scoutmaster, mentoring new scouts in leadership, safety, and outdoor skills such as fire building and tool use.
  • Gained experience in planning, communication, and project management through years of troop leadership roles.

Scouting has shaped how I approach challenges and teamwork. The lessons I learned through leadership and service continue to influence my approach to both personal growth and professional collaboration.
